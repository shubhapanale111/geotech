## 5. Four Core Practical Projects (Every Student Portfolio)

These four projects are built from scratch by every student using real-world open geospatial datasets:

### Project 1: Hospital Service Area & Multi-Ring Buffer Analysis
- **Domain:** Urban Healthcare Accessibility & Public Health
- **Tech Stack:** Python, GeoPandas, Shapely, Folium, QGIS
- **Problem Statement:**
  Emergency healthcare facilities must be reachable within critical time envelopes. Students analyze municipal hospital locations across Pune/Mumbai to calculate 500m, 1000m, and 3000m proximity zones, overlaying ward demographic data to identify underserved colonies and calculate healthcare coverage percentages.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Automated point buffer generation with attribute-driven distances
  - Spatial intersection with census population polygons
  - Underserved population disparity calculation
  - Interactive HTML web map + Publication-ready print layout

### Project 2: Drainage Network Extraction From DEM Data
- **Domain:** Hydrology, Watershed Management & Flood Prevention
- **Tech Stack:** 30m SRTM DEM, Rasterio, NumPy, PyProj, QGIS Hydrology
- **Problem Statement:**
  Natural water flow paths and catchments must be identified for water conservation and infrastructure drainage. Students process raw digital elevation models to extract natural river streams and delineate catchment basins.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Sink identification and depression filling (D8 algorithm)
  - Upstream flow direction and flow accumulation grid computation
  - Stream definition thresholding and Strahler stream order ranking
  - Catchment basin boundary vectorization and drainage density metrics

### Project 3: Land Use / Land Cover (LULC) Multi-Temporal Change Detection
- **Domain:** Environmental Monitoring, Forestry & Urban Sprawl
- **Tech Stack:** Sentinel-2 & Landsat 8/9 imagery, NumPy, Rasterio, Scikit-learn
- **Problem Statement:**
  Rapid urbanization often leads to forest loss and agricultural encroachment. Students compare satellite imagery from two distinct years (e.g. 2019 vs 2024) to evaluate land-use shifts.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Multi-band satellite radiometric correction and cloud masking
  - NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) and NDBI (Built-up Index) calculation
  - Supervised classification into Urban, Vegetation, Water, and Barren classes
  - Transition change matrix and percentage area change statistics

### Project 4: Flood Risk Susceptibility Mapping
- **Domain:** Disaster Management, Civil Infrastructure & Risk Mitigation
- **Tech Stack:** Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA), AHP, GeoPandas, QGIS
- **Problem Statement:**
  Combining multiple geographic layers to predict flood hazard zones and pinpoint exposed roads, bridges, and settlements before monsoon events.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) weight assignments for slope, elevation, river distance, and soil permeability
  - Weighted overlay synthesis into High, Medium, and Low risk zones
  - Infrastructure vulnerability overlay identifying critical roads and hospital access routes
  - Evacuation route accessibility guidance map
